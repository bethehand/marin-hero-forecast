# Addendum, 2026-10-22: end-of-run analysis plan, fixed before phase 2

Written on 2026-10-22, before the hero's phase 2 and before any eval inside it. The latest eval at writing is step 305,999.

Phase 2 is the switch to the cooldown data mix at step 312,192. The learning rate does not change course there. It has decayed linearly since warmup; it is at 24% of its peak at step 312,192 and reaches 5% at the end. [#8435](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/8435#issuecomment-5335872267) lists the linear schedule with min_lr_ratio 0.05, and a two-phase mixture whose phase 1 ends at 80% of steps.

This plan does not change or re-score v2, which was scored at the 16K switch under its condition 2. It fixes in advance what I will compute when the run ends and how I will read it.

## Question

**Primary: does the last 20% of training, from step 311,999 to the end, lower the loss as much as the scaling ladder predicts?**
- The ladder predicts the drop from fraction-matched fits. The team's Aug 19 forecast and v2 both use this method.
- The rungs share the hero's schedule: linear learning-rate decay to 5% of peak, and a switch to a phase-2 mix at about 80% of steps. The H100 new-mix rungs use the hero's three mixtures exactly; the GB200 rungs' mixtures differ.
- Whether the fitted law has a floor matters much less for the predicted drop than for the predicted level (see the table below). So the realized drop mainly tests whether the ladder's last-20% drop carries over to 337× and 1,890× the compute of its largest rungs.
- The public forecasts disagree on this:
  - The ladder-based ones expect the official macro to fall by 0.046–0.051 over this stretch.
  - Forecast A, [posted in #8435](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/8435#issuecomment-6328613653) on 2026-10-21, is 1.979 at 16K. That is about 0.07 below the latest eval (2.048 at step 305,999).

**Secondary:** where the final value lands relative to the public forecasts, on a comparable basis.

## Data and metrics

- **Evals:** W&B `marin-community/marin_moe`, in the hero's lineage (`hero-ctx16k-step257k` and any successor). They run every 3,000 steps and have been packed at 16K since step 257,999.
- **Start S:** a straight line fitted to the five evals at steps 299,999–311,999, read at step 311,999 (the last eval before phase 2). On M16, these five evals span PTB's recovery from its dip at steps 296,999–302,999, so S on M16 can be off by a few thousandths, and so can forecast A's implied drop. M14 and M15 are unaffected.
- **End Y:** the last eval logged in the lineage at step 387,000 or later. If the run completes, this should be a final eval at step 390,250 (p = 1), since every completed ladder rung also evaluated at its last step. Otherwise it is the eval at step 389,999.
- **Drop Δ = S − Y**, computed for:
  - **M14 (primary):** the mean loss over the 14 Paloma subsets other than PTB and WikiText-103. PTB has a confirmed contamination source, and WikiText-103 has one-sided drops that look like one ([#9874](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/9874)). PTB alone moved the macro by 0.017 between steps 293,999 and 299,999.
  - **M15:** everything except PTB (v2's companion target).
  - **M16:** the official macro, as logged. It is secondary because PTB events dominate its noise.
  - **The UncheatableEval macro:** reported, with no ladder prediction.
- **Noise:**
  - Detrended SD over the 17 evals at 16K up to step 305,999, taking the larger of linear and quadratic detrending: M14 0.0017, M15 0.0021, M16 0.0053, UncheatableEval 0.0014.
  - S is read from a five-point line, so its SE is 0.775 of one eval's noise. Y is a single eval. The SD of Δ is therefore that noise × √1.6: 0.0022 for M14, 0.0026 for M15 and 0.0067 for M16.

- **How the ladder rows are built:**
  - v2's machinery: the GB200 and H100 new-mix ladders weighted 0.75/0.25, both compute conventions, and the d2048 profiles as in v2.
  - Only the law is used. There is no anchoring to the hero, no drift term, no method error and no distance-bias allowance.
  - M16 is fitted to the logged macro directly.
- **Reading the cells:**
  - The first number is the weighted mean over the members. The brackets are their weighted 5–95% range, not a predictive interval.
  - Without a floor there are 8 equally weighted members, so the brackets are their full range.
  - The H100 new-mix ladder, which shares the hero's mixtures, is also reported on its own (`by_ladder` in the JSON).
- **The floor matters far more for the level than for the drop.** Before anchoring to the hero, the law with a floor puts the M16 end level at 2.045 and the law without one at 1.861, a gap of 0.18. Their drops differ by 0.004.
- **v2's rows** are the drop between v2's frozen median paths at the two points.
  - On M15 that is 0.002 above the law-only row, almost all from v2's distance-bias allowance; its drift term adds −0.0001.
  - The M16 path is 0.003 above the M15 path because it also carries PTB's expected change, including the allowance for the read at step 362,280.
- **The team's row** uses the three-decimal W&B values. Re-running the Aug 19 method without rounding gives 0.0465.
- **Forecast A** publishes an end value and a 90% interval, not a path. Its implied drop will be computed once S is known: S on M16 − 1.979.

## How the result will be read (fixed now)

The band for each metric is the union of the four ladder rows' 5–95% ranges, widened on each side by 2 SD of Δ.

| Metric | Ladder union | Band |
|---|---|---|
| M14 (primary) | [0.0401, 0.0502] | **[0.0357, 0.0546]** |
| M15 | [0.0409, 0.0542] | [0.0357, 0.0594] |
| M16 | [0.0408, 0.0573] | [0.0275, 0.0707] |

- **Inside the band:** Δ is consistent with the ladder's last-20% drop carrying over to this scale.
- **Outside the band:** the last 20% delivered more than the ladder predicts (above) or less (below).
- **What the band can't separate:** 16K-specific effects. A 20% drift in the 4K − 16K offset would by itself move Δ by about 0.006, more than the 2-SD noise margin on M14 (0.0044). A gap of 0.02 would need the offset to drift by about two-thirds of its size.
- **Which metric decides:** M14. M15 is read the same way as a check. M16 is reported, but its band is too wide to decide.

## Comparing the public forecasts (reported, not scored)

- **Forecasts that target the 16K eval** (forecast A 1.979, forecast B 1.935, and the straight-line baseline of 1.970 posted with them) are compared with Y on M16 as logged.
- **Forecasts built on 4K evals** (team Aug 19 2.039, Sep 14 2.027 and Oct 3 2.100; v2 2.035) are compared with Y plus the 4K − 16K offset.
  - If Marin runs a 4K eval of the final checkpoint, that measured difference is used.
  - Otherwise, the offset measured at the switch is used. The switch arms restored step 252,000 and continued at 16K. Each offset is the mean of an arm's evals at steps 252,049 and 252,099, minus the hero's 4K eval at step 251,999:
    - `qk16k-a1-step252k` (`qk_mult` 1.48, the hero's setting after the switch): 0.0306 on M16, 0.0298 on M15 and 0.0297 on M14.
    - `qk16k-a0-step252k` (`qk_mult` 1.3, the hero's setting before the switch): 0.0283.
  - If a 4K eval of the final checkpoint exists, the offset's drift since the switch is reported: the measured offset minus 0.0306 on M16, or 0.0297 on M14. Δ net of the drift is given only as a sensitivity, since that assigns all of the drift since step 252,000 to the last 20%.

## Contingencies

- **The run stops before step 387,000, or its schedule or eval setup (subsets, packing) changes:** the analysis uses the last eval before the change, and the ladder prediction is taken at the same fraction.
- **A PTB or WikiText event in phase 2:** M14 is unaffected, and M16 is reported with a note. #9874 mapped PTB reads under the 4K data order. The dip at step 296,999 does not match that mapping, so the timing of any read in phase 2 is uncertain.
- **The lineage moves to a new W&B run:** evals are joined by step, keeping the newest run in the lineage, as in v2.

## Caveats

- The ladders were trained and evaluated at 4K. The hero has trained at 16K since step 257,553 and evaluates at 16K (see the reading rules above).
- Each rung's 80% eval comes 7–31 steps after its own switch to the phase-2 mix (GB200 d1536: 1 step before). S comes 193 steps before the hero's switch. So any immediate effect of the switch is in Δ, but not in the ladder rows.
- The d2048 rung stopped at 81%, so its last-20% drop is extrapolated from the smaller GB200 rungs.
- This is one run and one outcome.
- Contamination outside PTB and WikiText is not removed.

## Reproduce

```bash
cd code && python phase2_plan.py ../data/snapshot_2026-10-09T0440Z ../results/phase2
```

The script does two things:
- It reads the ladders from the frozen 2026-10-09 snapshot. All rungs had ended by then (d2048 stopped at 81%), so there is no new ladder data.
- It pulls the team's Aug 19 trajectory, the switch arms and the hero's 16K evals from W&B into `results/phase2/`.

Every number above that the script computes is in `results/phase2/plan_numbers.json`, including the unanchored end levels (`end_level_unanchored`). The noise and bands use the hero's 16K evals through step 305,999, so they reproduce after newer evals are logged.

## Amendment, 2026-10-22, before phase 2

After this plan was pushed, Larry replied on #8435 ([comment](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/8435#issuecomment-6336416457)) that Marin will re-evaluate all checkpoints at 4K once the run is done. He also noted that the hero trains at 4K, then 16K, then 65K. This amendment says how the re-evals will be used. It was made before any eval in phase 2.

- **If the 4K re-evals are published with per-subset losses, they carry the primary test.**
  - The same quantities are computed on them. S comes from a straight line fitted to the 4K re-evals of the checkpoints between steps 299,000 and 312,192, read at step 311,999. If fewer than three checkpoints fall in that range, S is the 4K re-eval of the last checkpoint before step 312,192, and the ladder predictions and bands are recomputed from that checkpoint's step. Y is the 4K re-eval of the final checkpoint of the 390,251-step schedule (p = 1).
  - Δ is computed on M14, M15 and M16 and read against the same bands. The noise of the re-evals is reported alongside.
  - The ladders were evaluated at 4K, so this basis needs no packing offset.
  - The 16K-logged Δ is still reported.
- **If the eval packing changes again during phase 2** (for example to 65K): the 16K-logged Δ stops at the last 16K eval, as the contingencies above say, and the 4K re-evals carry the primary test.
- **Public forecasts built on 4K evals** are compared with the final checkpoint's 4K re-eval directly.
- **One difference the re-evals do not remove,** as Larry points out: the hero trained at longer contexts and the ladders did not, so its 4K evals may differ slightly from those of a model that stayed at 4K.
